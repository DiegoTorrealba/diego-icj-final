About me

Frequently Asked Questions

I am a multimedia journalist and programmer who uses computational techniques to produce data-driven stories and visualizations, create tools for journalists, and explore the intersection between developing technologies and journalism.

This summer, I am interning as a Dow Jones News Fund data journalism intern at the University of Maryland’s Howard Center for Investigative Journalism, where I am learning how to use my data journalism skills for investigative reporting.

I graduated with high honors from UT Austin, where I received my bachelor’s in journalism and a certificate in computer science. As an undergrad, I produced video content and wrote newsletters for The Daily Texan, edited podcast scripts for The Drag Audio Production House, and worked on data projects as a student technician and data fellow for the Media Innovation Group.

Raised by Venezuelan parents, I am a first-generation American from Texas. I spend the majority of my time in Austin, but I also am frequently in Dallas and Houston.

When applying for college, I struggled to choose a major to consistently apply with because various degrees interested me in one way or another. Every choice made me feel as though I had to either sacrifice my love for reading and writing or my love for technology and science. So, when I had the option to apply for both a primary and backup major, I decided to apply for both computer science and journalism, and I was acceepted subsequently into Moody College of Communication's journalism program.

For some reason, I thought that this meant I would never look at code again or be able to combine my interests in any meaningful way. It was not until I began to study journalism further, however, that I realized an entire data journalism community existed of people like me who loved both storytelling and programming. Since then, I have sought to learn as much as I can about how I can use data analysis and computational techniques to augment different forms of news content to feel more engaging and authentic for audiences.

Comparing contracting with minority-owned businesses in Texas and California between the Trump and Biden administrations

Jun 5, 2025 | Story Pitch

I wrote a formal pitch as part of my Dow Jones News Fund internship training that analyzed federal government spending data to compare contracts awarded to minority-owned businesses in Texas and California between the Trump and Biden administrations. For this pitch, I analyzed available data to identify noteworthy trends and minority-owned businesses to investigate, noted important holes in the available data, and preliminarily showed how the similarity in spending between the two administrations is greater than one might assume.

"Americans and their data" Pew Research Center Analysis

Apr 28, 2025 | Website

For my final project in my Reporting with Data class, I used survey data from Wave 127 of Pew Research Center’s American Trends Panel (ATP), which focuses on “Americans and their data.” Using the survey’s questionnaire and methodology documents, I cleaned and organized the data to create multiple ggplot visualizations and ultimately write an AP-style news summary of my findings. For this assignment, I cleaned, analyzed, and visualized the data using R and R libraries such as Tidyverse, ultimately uploading the website via QuartoPub.
